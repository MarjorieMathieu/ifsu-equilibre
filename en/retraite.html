What we practise

Not a tasting menu. A structured programme, built on 50 years of IFSU transmission. Each practice has a function. Together, they form something coherent.

◎

IFSU Vedic meditation

The central technique. Personal mantra, seated practice, gradual inner silence.

◎

Yoga & personal practices

Sequences adapted to each body. Postures, breathing, stretching — concrete, not spectacle.

◎

Chanting & gong meditation

Group mantra chanting. Sound concentration. A tool for presence that many people discover here for the first time — and take home with them.

◎

Gururaj satsangs

Listening to audio/video recordings of Gururaj Ananda Yogi followed by group discussion. The philosophical heart of the retreat.

◎

Yoga Nidra & deep relaxation

Guided relaxation of the layers of the body. Visualisation, Tratak (candle gazing), relaxation of the chakras. The body learns to let go without effort.

◎

Silent walk

Every morning, before the practices. The green setting of Koningsteen. Shared silence creates something that words describe poorly.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to know how to meditate before coming?

No. Zero prerequisites. Complete beginners are welcomed with a separate briefing on arrival. If you already have a practice, it won't be ignored either.

Is the retreat religious?

No. IFSU meditation rests on techniques — not on a belief. The satsangs of Gururaj Ananda Yogi address fundamental questions (meaning, consciousness, everyday life), but without dogma or any obligation to adhere. You can practise without believing anything in particular.

How does the accommodation work?

Single and shared rooms are available at Koningsteen. The room type is specified at registration. Everything is included in the price — accommodation, meals, programme.

Is the retreat in French or English?

Both. Margy leads in French, Breeyana in English (and French). Group sessions are bilingual. No problem if you don't speak both.

Can I come for just one day?

Yes, on Tuesday, Wednesday or Thursday, subject to availability, for €150. Arrive at 08:45. The package includes the day's programme and meals, that night's accommodation and breakfast the following morning. The full stay offers more time to settle in and practise over several days.

Are there any discounts?

No. €750 is the single, fixed price. It covers five days of programme, accommodation and all meals. It doesn't move.
