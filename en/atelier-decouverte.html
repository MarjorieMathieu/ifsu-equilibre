The real problem

It isn't you.
It's that you were left without a guide.

◆You never know whether your posture is right, so doubt sets in and you give up.
◆You're afraid of “doing it wrong”, of never managing to calm your thoughts.
◆Alone at home, nobody is there to correct what's sticking, or to tell you it's normal.

Here: a method, daily follow-up, a group of five.

The method

A format designed so we move forward together.

You are not left on your own after the first workshop: an audio every day, a follow-up notebook, concrete answers to the blocks you meet. This is the common foundation of the in-person formats.

Small-group workshops

We learn the posture, correct what's blocking, practise side by side. The number and rhythm of sessions depend on the format chosen.

Daily audio micro-sessions

To keep the thread at home, day after day, without spending hours on it.

The 15-Day Follow-up Notebook

One page a day to note what you feel, what sticks, what moves forward. It's what turns the trial into a habit.

The “Anti-Blocks” Practical Guide

Answers to the most common blocks — legs going numb, the mind racing, discouragement on day 4 — so you're never stuck alone.

When it's uncomfortable

Sometimes something unpleasant comes up: fear, anger, inner tension. The IFSU tradition sees this as a release of tension. We don't resist it, we don't judge ourselves, and if needed we shorten the session. It's an adjustment, not a failure.

A method that is taught, not a relaxation session

What you learn comes from IFSU Vedic meditation, which I have taught since 2014. I give you the method, then you practise it: I advise, I unblock, you sit.

5 people maximum per group

Because at that size I really see each person, and everyone can ask their questions without feeling in the way.

The programme

The same content, whichever format you choose.

01
The foundations
Sitting without pain, defusing what we think we know about meditation, getting used to your breath.
02
Body and senses
Aligning your spine, no longer being distracted by the noises around you, releasing through the body scan.
03
Mind and independence
Stopping judging yourself in your practice, discovering the sound OM, meditating alone with confidence.
What you will be able to do at the end

Practise at home, with nobody beside you, a complete meditation of about ten minutes. Spot what's blocking you. Pick up again after a missed day.

What you take away

  • A practice you can repeat on your own, at home.
  • 15 audios to accompany you day after day.
  • A follow-up notebook so that the trial becomes a habit.
  • A guide to the most common blocks: legs going numb, the mind racing, discouragement on day 4.
  • A group of 5: your questions get answers that fit you.
  • After the programme: a group get-together at the Studio, by registration, to keep the thread.
